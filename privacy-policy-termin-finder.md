# Privacy Policy — Termin Finder

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026
**Last updated:** 17 November 2026
**Application:** Termin Finder (Android package `de.jied.terminfinder`)

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR is:
> Email: **forgottenfork2025@gmail.com**

If you have any question about this policy or about your data, write to the address above. We answer
data-protection requests within 30 days.

## 2. The short version

Termin Finder watches the public appointment pages of service.berlin.de and notifies you when new free
appointment slots appear. Everything you set up in the app is stored **on your own device**.

There is **no Termin Finder server and no account**. The app contains **no analytics, no crash reporting
and no advertising**. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Termin Finder is an unofficial app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Land Berlin or
service.berlin.de.

## 3. What data the app processes

### 3.1 Data stored on your device

| Data | Examples | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Monitoring tasks | Service ID or name, selected offices, wanted dates, polling interval | Private app storage on your device |
| Check history | Time and result of each check, appointment dates found | Private app storage on your device |
| Catalog cache | List of services and offices downloaded from service.berlin.de | Private app storage on your device |
| Settings | Whether notifications about new dates are enabled | Private app storage on your device |

None of this data identifies you. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any
other personal details.

### 3.2 Requests to service.berlin.de

To do its job, the app connects directly from your device to **service.berlin.de**, the public website of
the Land Berlin, to:

- download the list of services and offices,
- check the appointment calendar for the services you monitor.

These are ordinary HTTPS page requests, the same as when you open the website in a browser. Like any web
server, service.berlin.de receives your **IP address**, the requested page (which contains the service and
office IDs you monitor) and standard request headers such as the browser identifier and language. The app
does not log in and does not send any personal data to the website.

Processing of this data by service.berlin.de is governed by the
[privacy policy of service.berlin.de](https://service.berlin.de/terminvereinbarung/datenschutzerklaerung/). We have no access to it.

When you tap **"Go to booking"**, the booking page opens in your browser. Booking itself takes place on
service.berlin.de, outside this app.

### 3.3 Data we do not collect

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not access your
location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Internet | To load pages from service.berlin.de |
| Notifications | To tell you when new appointment dates appear |
| Exact alarms | To run checks at the interval you set |
| Foreground service, wake lock | To complete a check in the background without being interrupted |
| Ignore battery optimisations (request) | So that the system does not delay or stop background checks |

## 5. Why we process data, and on what legal basis

The data in section 3.1 is processed solely on your device to provide the app's core function, monitoring
appointments for you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive it.

## 6. Who receives your data

We do **not** share data with third parties. The only party the app communicates with is
**service.berlin.de** (section 3.2). App distribution is handled by **Google Play** under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 7. How long data is kept

All data stays on your device until you delete a task in the app, clear the app's data
(*Android Settings → Apps → Termin Finder → Storage → Clear data*) or uninstall the app.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and to lodge a complaint with a
supervisory authority (Art. 77). Because we hold no data about you, most of these rights are exercised
directly in the app. For any question, write to the address in section 1.

## 9. Children

Termin Finder is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. All connections to
service.berlin.de use HTTPS/TLS. We hold no copy of your data, so there is no central database of ours that
could be breached.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at the URL
published on the app's Google Play listing. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current revision.
